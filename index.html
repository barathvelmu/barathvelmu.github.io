Research

My research so far spans generative modeling of digital twins, behavioral simulation using large language models, and token entanglement in language models. I am interested in how large language models reason about human behaviour, and what their hidden representations mean for interpretability and safety! My recent work includes synthetic customer modeling at Anheuser-Busch InBev through MIT, and independent work on how models pass along preferences through data that seem meaningless.

Papers

Subliminal Prompting Beyond Static Geometry: Causal Depth and Multi-Token Confounds
Barath Velmurugan
arXiv preprint (cs.CL), July 2026
Separates four measurements of token entanglement (output co-variation, output-vector alignment, hidden-state readability, causal control) in a fixed animal-number prompting protocol. From Llama-3.1-8B to 70B the fixed output-vector account predicts behavior less well, while transferring a hidden state controls the answer earlier in the network; a Qwen multi-token confound is resolved through total covariance.

Kaetram Arena

Teaches a small open model (Qwen 2B) to play a persistent open-world MMORPG through long-horizon, tool-mediated actions, with teacher and student sharing one typed 17-tool API.
